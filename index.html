About Adarsh Lakefront

Adarsh Lakefront overlooks Saulkere Lake, a 50-acre haven for bird and aquatic life, in Bellandur, on Outer Ring Road next to Intel. Adarsh Group planned the 5.5-acre community with 70% open space, a lagoon pool, a tree plaza and a skating rink.

The homes are ready to move in. Choose from 513 apartments across 3 towers of 2 basements, ground and 14 floors: 3 bedroom homes of 1,915 to 2,235 sq ft, with a spacious clubhouse at the centre.
